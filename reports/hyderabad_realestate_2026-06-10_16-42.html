Overall Hyderabad Market — Sustained Momentum Amid Consolidation

: Hyderabad's residential real estate market in June 2026 continues its multi-year bull run, recording a city-wide average price appreciation of 11.4% year-on-year. Total registered transactions in May 2026 reached 7,842 units, a 9.2% uptick over May 2025, driven by sustained IT sector hiring and robust NRI demand. The market has absorbed the state government's revised guidance value hike of 8% (effective March 2026) with minimal cooling, reflecting deep structural demand. New launches in Q2 2026 numbered 14,300 units across the metro area, with absorption rates holding strong at 74%, signaling healthy inventory equilibration despite elevated supply.

Western IT Corridor — The Engine Room of Price Discovery

: The Financial District–Nanakramguda–Kokapet stretch remains the city's most dynamic micro-market, with average transaction prices touching ₹10,200–₹12,800 per sq ft for premium mid-rise and high-rise projects. Kokapet's Neopolis layout has seen plotted values breach ₹1.05 lakh per sq yard, a 22% surge year-on-year, buoyed by the upcoming ORR Phase-III interchange. Gachibowli and Kondapur command ₹8,500–₹10,500 per sq ft for 3BHK configurations, while Manikonda and Narsingi are emerging as value plays at ₹7,200–₹8,800 per sq ft. The corridor's proximity to marquee tech campuses — TCS Synergy Park, Microsoft, and the newly operational Google Hyderabad Phase-II — continues to underwrite rental yields averaging 3.1–3.6%, making it attractive for both end-users and investors.

Premium Banjara Hills & Jubilee Hills — Scarcity Premium Intact

: Supply constraints continue to define the Banjara Hills–Jubilee Hills luxury belt, where sub-1,000 unit new launches annually keep prices elevated. Luxury villas and ultra-premium apartments in Road Nos. 36 and 45, Banjara Hills, transacted at ₹18,000–₹24,000 per sq ft in Q1 2026 — up 13.7% year-on-year. Jubilee Hills luxury boutique projects by developers like My Home, Aparna, and Prestige (post their Hyderabad foray) are fully sold out at launch, with resale premiums of 6–9% over original booking prices. The segment's clientele skews toward HNIs, CXO-level professionals, and the diaspora, ensuring price resilience even in broader market softness cycles. Rental demand for furnished 4BHK+ units in this belt is near full occupancy, with monthly rents ranging ₹1.5–₹3.8 lakh.

Mid-Segment North & West — Healthy Volume Backbone

: Kukatpally, Bachupally, Miyapur, and Kompally together account for approximately 31% of total city transaction volumes, functioning as the market's volume engine. Prices in Bachupally and Miyapur have consolidated at ₹5,800–₹7,200 per sq ft following a sharp 14% run-up in 2024–25, with developers offering subvention schemes and 3-year price locks to maintain momentum. The MMTS Phase-II corridor activation through Kukatpally has boosted transit-oriented demand, with projects within 500 meters of proposed stations commanding a 7–10% premium. Kompally along NH-44 continues to attract first-time homebuyers from the pharma and manufacturing sectors, with 2BHK units in the ₹55–₹75 lakh range moving briskly. The mid-segment is also witnessing strong co-living conversions in older inventory, moderating resale supply in some sub-localities.

Emerging Corridors — Adibatla, Tukkuguda & Shadnagar Gain Traction

: South-eastern Hyderabad's aerospace and defence manufacturing belt — anchored by Adibatla SEZ and the Fab City expansion — is the fastest-growing emerging corridor in 2026. Adibatla plotted developments have jumped from ₹22,000 per sq yard (2024) to ₹34,000–₹38,000 per sq yard, a 58% two-year appreciation. Tukkuguda and Ibrahimpatnam, lying along the Pharma City influence zone, are recording apartment launches at ₹4,200–₹5,500 per sq ft with aggressive pre-launch subscriptions. Shadnagar on NH-44 has benefitted from the HMDA master plan's industrial zone notification, triggering a 19% price spike in the last three quarters. Mokila in the western growth corridor, post the Outer Ring Road spur development, is attracting plotted villa project launches by Ramky, NSL, and Signature Developers at ₹42,000–₹58,000 per sq yard.

Investment Recommendations — Where Capital Finds Best Risk-Adjusted Returns

: For June 2026, the highest conviction buys are plotted developments in Kokapet and Mokila for long-horizon (5–7 year) capital appreciation plays, targeting 15–18% CAGR. For rental yield-focused investors, 2BHK and 3BHK apartments in Nanakramguda and Raidurgam within ₹90 lakh–₹1.4 crore offer the optimal yield-appreciation balance at 3.2–3.8% gross yield. Adibatla remains a speculative but high-upside bet for risk-tolerant investors with a 3–5 year horizon, contingent on Pharma City activation timelines. Investors are advised to avoid over-supplied micro-markets including parts of Pocharam and Ghatkesar where new supply has outpaced absorption, leaving 18–24 month inventory overhangs. Overall, Hyderabad's fundamentals — IT job creation, infrastructure capex, and favourable state policies — sustain a constructive 12-month outlook with city-wide price appreciation expected at 9–12% through June 2027.
