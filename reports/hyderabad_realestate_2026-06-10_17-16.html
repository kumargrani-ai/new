Overall Market Dynamics — June 2026

: Hyderabad's residential real estate market continues its robust upward trajectory in June 2026, recording a 13.5% year-on-year appreciation in average prices across the city. The market has absorbed approximately 48,750 active listings, with absorption rates running at roughly 62% on a quarterly basis — the highest since Q4 2023. New launch volumes in H1 2026 stood at around 31,200 units, led predominantly by mid-premium and luxury segments. Infrastructure catalysts including the Hyderabad Metro Phase-2 (Jubilee Hills–HITEC City link) and the Outer Ring Road densification have been primary drivers of renewed buyer confidence across multiple micro-markets.

Western IT Corridor — HITEC City to Kokapet

: The Financial District and Kokapet pocket remain the single hottest cluster in the city, with average capital values now touching ₹12,800–₹14,200 per sq. ft. for new launches. Gachibowli commands ₹12,500/sq. ft., Kondapur has crossed ₹11,000/sq. ft., and Madhapur hovers around ₹11,400/sq. ft. Kokapet, anchored by the Neopolis Township and the upcoming ORR commercial belt, saw a 19.2% YoY surge — making it the single hottest locality in June 2026. Corporate leasing activity from GCCs (Global Capability Centres) and tech majors at Raheja Mindspace and DLF Cybercity continues to underpin rental yields of 3.8–4.4% in this corridor, sustaining end-user and investor demand simultaneously.

Premium Segment — Banjara Hills and Jubilee Hills

: Banjara Hills Road No. 36 and Road No. 12 micro-markets have consolidated luxury pricing at ₹17,500–₹22,000 per sq. ft. for boutique developments, while Jubilee Hills Road No. 45 and Film Nagar see comparable transactions averaging ₹15,800/sq. ft. Ultra-luxury villa plots on the Banjara Hills–Jubilee Hills arterial belt are now quoted at ₹1.2–₹2.1 crore per plot (200–300 sq. yd.), having appreciated roughly 11% over June 2025 levels. Supply in this segment is inherently constrained, with only 14 new premium projects launched in H1 2026, keeping vacancy pressure negligible and resale premiums intact.

Mid-Segment Markets — Kukatpally to Kompally

: Kukatpally KPHB Colony remains the city's largest mid-segment hub at ₹7,600/sq. ft., with strong demand from salaried professionals in the ₹70 lakh–₹1.2 crore ticket-size bracket. Miyapur and Bachupally have appreciated to ₹6,900 and ₹6,400/sq. ft. respectively, buoyed by the Metro Blue Line extension opening in February 2026. Kompally and Shamirpet on the northern periphery are emerging as affordable alternatives, averaging ₹5,800–₹6,100/sq. ft., with new township projects by Aparna, Prestige, and SMR attracting first-time buyers priced out of western markets. Rental yields in mid-segment zones remain healthy at 3.2–3.6%.

Emerging Micro-Markets — Adibatla and Mokila

: The southeastern corridor anchored by Adibatla (Aerospace and Defence SEZ) has recorded the sharpest volume growth in H1 2026, with new project launches up 38% versus the same period last year. Capital values in Adibatla stand at ₹4,600/sq. ft., while Tukkuguda commands ₹4,900/sq. ft. and Ibrahimpatnam plots are trading at ₹2,800–₹3,400/sq. yd. On the western fringe, Mokila and Tellapur have reached ₹5,400/sq. ft., driven by spillover from HITEC City. Shadnagar on NH-44 has matured as a plotted development hub, with HMDA-approved layouts transacting at ₹1,800–₹2,600/sq. yd., posting 22% YoY appreciation.

Investment Recommendations — June 2026 Outlook

: For capital appreciation, Kokapet and Adibatla offer the strongest 3-year return potential, estimated at 18–22% CAGR based on committed infrastructure pipelines. Mid-segment investors should target Miyapur and Bachupally for steady rental yields of 3.4%+ given Metro-linked demand. Luxury investors with a 5-year horizon should consider sub-₹10 crore boutique flats in Jubilee Hills, where new supply remains scarce. Plotted development in Shadnagar and Tukkuguda suits risk-tolerant investors seeking land appreciation. As a caution, Gachibowli and Madhapur presale inventories have risen 14% QoQ, suggesting near-term price consolidation in that sub-market; buyers there should negotiate 6–8% below ask on under-construction units launched before Q3 2025.
