Overall Market Overview

: Hyderabad's residential real estate market sustained strong momentum through Q2 2026, with citywide average prices reaching ₹9,250 per sq ft — a 13.4% year-on-year increase driven by robust IT sector hiring, infrastructure completions on the Regional Ring Road (RRR), and sustained NRI demand. Total active listings stood at approximately 24,800 units, with absorption rates of 68% in premium micro-markets. New launch volumes in January–May 2026 crossed 18,200 units, up from 15,400 during the same period in 2025, reflecting developer confidence despite elevated input costs.

Western IT Corridor (Gachibowli–HITEC City–Madhapur–Kondapur)

: This belt remains the engine of Hyderabad's residential story, averaging ₹11,800–₹14,200 per sq ft for new launches. Gachibowli recorded the highest transaction volumes in the corridor at 3,450 registrations in Q1 2026 alone. Kokapet has emerged as the breakout micro-market, with prices jumping from ₹8,400 to ₹10,600 per sq ft over 18 months, fuelled by the Financial District expansion and three new Grade-A office completions totalling 4.2 million sq ft. Demand for 3BHK units in the ₹1.2–₹1.8 crore range remains severely undersupplied, pushing delivery timelines to 36+ months.

Premium Banjara Hills and Jubilee Hills Segment

: Luxury and ultra-luxury inventory in Banjara Hills (Road Nos. 10–14) and Jubilee Hills held firm at ₹18,500–₹26,000 per sq ft for branded developer projects. The market saw eight transactions above ₹10 crore in May 2026 alone, predominantly penthouses and villas. Jubilee Hills' supply pipeline remains intentionally thin due to land scarcity, supporting a 9.8% price appreciation YoY. Row-villa communities along Banjara Hills Road No. 12 clocked ₹5.2–₹7.8 crore ticket sizes, with near-zero unsold inventory in completed projects.

Mid-Segment Localities (Miyapur, Bachupally, Kompally, Manikonda)

: The ₹55 lakh–₹90 lakh ticket-size segment saw the highest unit absorption in volume terms, accounting for 44% of all registrations citywide. Miyapur and Bachupally combined for 5,100 registrations in H1 2026, driven by metro Phase-II connectivity and proximity to HITEC City via NH-65. Manikonda prices rose to ₹6,200–₹7,100 per sq ft from ₹5,400 a year prior. Kompally, benefiting from the Outer Ring Road and expanding pharma/manufacturing clusters to the north, saw new launches priced at ₹5,800–₹6,500 per sq ft with brisk sales velocity of under 90 days from launch.

Emerging and Peripheral Growth Corridors

: Mokila and Tellapur on the western periphery are attracting plotted-development investors, with HMDA-approved layouts transacting at ₹28,000–₹42,000 per sq yard — a 22% jump from June 2025. Shamshabad–Rajendranagar, catalysed by the new Hyderabad Metro Rail Airport Line extension announcement, recorded a 31% spike in site registrations over Q1 2026. Adibatla in the eastern corridor, home to the aerospace and defence manufacturing SEZ, is seeing apartment launches at ₹4,900–₹5,600 per sq ft and is forecast to breach the ₹6,500 mark by Q4 2026 as Tata Aerospace and DRDO campus phases commission.

Investment Recommendations

: For capital appreciation, Kokapet and Tellapur offer the best risk-adjusted upside over a 3–5 year horizon, with projected 18–22% CAGR supported by office supply and RRR-linked connectivity. Investors seeking rental yield should prioritise Gachibowli and Kondapur, where 2BHK units yield 3.8–4.4% annually given sustained IT workforce rentals. The luxury segment in Jubilee Hills suits long-horizon HNI buyers given supply constraints. Caution is advised in heavily over-supplied pockets such as Nallagandla, where unsold inventory has crossed 28 months of absorption, and selective due diligence on RERA compliance and builder track record remains essential across all segments.
