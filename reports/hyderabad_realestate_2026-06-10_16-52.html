Overall Market Outlook

: Hyderabad's residential real estate market in June 2026 continues its robust growth trajectory, recording an average price appreciation of 13.4% year-on-year across all micro-markets. The city's total active listings stand at approximately 52,340 units, reflecting healthy supply-demand equilibrium. Residential absorption rates have improved by 18% compared to June 2025, driven primarily by IT sector hiring resumption and sustained NRI demand. The Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority (HMDA) zones recorded over ₹28,400 crore in registered transactions in Q1 2026, a 22% jump from Q1 2025, underscoring investor confidence in the market's structural fundamentals.

Western IT Corridor — Engine of Growth

: The Financial District–Kokapet–Nanakramguda belt remains Hyderabad's most dynamic growth cluster, with average prices touching ₹11,200–₹13,800 per sqft for new launches as of June 2026. Kokapet alone saw 14 new project launches in H1 2026, adding approximately 8,200 units to the pipeline. Global capability centers (GCCs) — now numbering over 210 in the city — continue to anchor demand in Gachibowli and Madhapur, where Grade-A office absorption crossed 7.4 million sqft in the trailing twelve months. The Outer Ring Road (ORR) proximity premium has widened to nearly 28% versus comparable non-ORR locations, cementing the corridor's investment case.

Premium Segment — Banjara Hills & Jubilee Hills

: Banjara Hills (Roads 10, 12, and 36) and Jubilee Hills command ₹16,500–₹22,000 per sqft for boutique luxury developments, representing a 9.7% YoY increase. Supply here remains deliberately constrained — fewer than 1,100 luxury units (₹5 crore+) were launched citywide in H1 2026 — sustaining seller leverage in negotiations. Ultra-luxury penthouses on Road 45, Jubilee Hills, and along Banjara Hills Road 12 are transacting at ₹24,000–₹28,500 per sqft with sub-45-day absorption timelines. High-net-worth demand from pharma promoters, IT CXOs, and returning NRIs from the Gulf and US is keeping unsold inventory at a multi-year low of just 6.2 months in these sub-markets.

Mid-Segment — The Volume Driver

: Localities such as Kondapur, Manikonda, Puppalaguda, and Tellapur are driving the bulk of transactional volume, averaging ₹6,800–₹8,400 per sqft for 2BHK and 3BHK configurations in the ₹75 lakh–₹1.5 crore ticket-size band. Tellapur in particular has emerged as a standout performer, recording 31% price appreciation over the past 24 months on the back of improved ORR connectivity and proximity to Aerospace Park. Kukatpally Housing Board Colony (KPHB) and Bachupally continue attracting first-time homebuyers with prices in the ₹5,200–₹6,500 per sqft range, supported by metro Phase-II connectivity improvements completed in early 2026.

Emerging Micro-Markets — Next Wave of Appreciation

: Adibatla, Tukkuguda, and the Fab City–Aerospace corridor on the eastern periphery are witnessing institutional developer attention for the first time, with land rates doubling to ₹18,000–₹24,000 per square yard since 2023. Mokila and Shankarpally on the western fringe are attracting plotted development demand, with HMDA-approved layouts moving at ₹28,000–₹38,000 per square yard. Shadnagar, 55 km south along the Mumbai Highway, is consolidating its position as a logistics and warehousing hub, generating residential demand in the ₹3,500–₹4,800 per sqft range. Ghatkesar and Pocharam near the Data Centre Park in east Hyderabad are also gaining traction as data-centre-driven employment migrates eastward.

Investment Recommendations

: For capital appreciation over a 3–5 year horizon, Kokapet, Nanakramguda, and Tellapur offer the best risk-adjusted returns, with projected CAGR of 14–17% supported by confirmed infrastructure spends and GCC expansion pipelines. Rental yield seekers should focus on Gachibowli and Madhapur 2BHK units priced ₹90 lakh–₹1.2 crore, where gross rental yields of 3.8–4.5% remain above the city average. Plotted development in Mokila and Shankarpally suits patient capital with a 5–7 year hold, given the government's stated intent to extend the metro to Shankarpally by 2029. Investors should remain cautious in over-supplied pockets of Miyapur and Bachupally, where unsold inventory overhang exceeds 22 months and new launches continue despite muted absorption.
