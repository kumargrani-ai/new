Overall Market Overview

: Hyderabad's residential real estate market in June 2026 continues its robust upward trajectory, registering a year-on-year price appreciation of 14.2% across key micromarkets. The city recorded approximately 18,400 active residential listings this month, with absorption rates touching 68% in premium corridors. HMDA-approved layouts and RERA-registered projects dominate buyer preference, with new supply additions of roughly 9,200 units in Q2 2026 still falling short of demand, particularly in the sub-₹80 lakh segment. Transaction volumes on SRO data indicate a 22% jump in registrations compared to June 2025, driven by end-user demand from the IT/ITeS workforce and NRI investment inflows.

Western IT Corridor Dominance

: The Financial District–Nanakramguda–Kokapet–Gachibowli belt remains Hyderabad's highest-velocity micromarket, with average prices now at ₹9,200–₹11,500 per sq ft for new launches. Kokapet, buoyed by Google's expanded campus and multiple Fortune 500 office completions along the Outer Ring Road, saw prices cross ₹10,800 per sq ft for 3BHK configurations in premium towers. Gachibowli's rental yields are holding firm at 3.8–4.2%, making it an attractive buy-to-let proposition. Tellapur and Nallagandla are absorbing spillover demand at ₹6,800–₹8,200 per sq ft, with inventory clearing in under 45 days for projects by Prestige, Lodha, and Godrej Properties.

Premium Banjara Hills and Jubilee Hills Segment

: The classic luxury enclaves of Banjara Hills (Roads 10, 12, 36) and Jubilee Hills continue to command aspirational premiums, with villa plots trading at ₹1.8–₹3.5 lakh per sq yd and apartment projects touching ₹14,000–₹18,000 per sq ft for ultra-luxury configurations. Supply is deliberately constrained, with only 4–5 boutique project launches recorded in H1 2026. Madhapur and Jubilee Hills Road No. 45 corridor are seeing renewed HNI interest in duplex penthouses averaging ₹6–₹12 crore. Secondary market transactions in these localities are brisk, with motivated NRI sellers offset by strong domestic HNI buyer demand, keeping prices sticky on the upside.

Mid-Segment Resilience

: Areas like Miyapur, Bachupally, Nizampet, Kompally, and Uppal are where genuine mid-income demand is playing out at ₹4,500–₹6,500 per sq ft. Miyapur remains among the highest-volume localities by unit count, with over 1,100 active listings and average ticket sizes of ₹55–₹75 lakh for 2BHK units. Bachupally's improved connectivity via the expanded NH-65 access road and the proposed metro Phase-III alignment has pushed prices up 11.8% year-on-year to ₹5,400 per sq ft. Developers such as My Home, Aparna, and SMR are actively launching in these pockets, offering subvention schemes to sustain momentum among salaried buyers with home loan eligibility constraints.

Emerging Growth Corridors

: Pharma City's ecosystem around Genome Valley (Shamirpet, Gundlapochampally) and the Aerospace SEZ near Adibatla are catalyzing entirely new residential clusters. Adibatla prices have jumped from ₹3,100 to ₹4,600 per sq ft over 18 months, fuelled by TATA Advanced Systems, Bharat Forge, and DRDO campus activity. On the northern arc, Shamirpet and Medchal are attracting plotted development investors at ₹28,000–₹45,000 per sq yd, with annualised appreciation of 16–19%. Patancheru and Sultanpur along the Pune Highway are also emerging as warehousing-adjacent residential nodes, targeting blue-collar and mid-management workforce housing below ₹40 lakh.

Investment Recommendations

: For capital appreciation, Kokapet and Narsingi offer the strongest 3-year outlook given confirmed office supply of 18 million sq ft under construction in the Financial District micro-zone. For rental income, 2BHK units in Gachibowli and Kondapur priced at ₹75–₹90 lakh generate net yields of 3.9–4.3% with minimal vacancy risk. Plotted development in Adibatla and Tukkuguda presents a high-risk, high-reward play with 5-year IRRs potentially exceeding 18%, contingent on Pharma City Phase-II activation. Investors should avoid over-leveraged positions in Outer Ring Road periphery layouts beyond 35 km from Hitec City, where infrastructure delivery timelines remain uncertain. RERA compliance, clear land titles, and proximity to announced metro corridors should be non-negotiable screening criteria in the current cycle.
