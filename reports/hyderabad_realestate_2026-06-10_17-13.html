Overall Market Overview

: Hyderabad's residential real estate market in June 2026 continues its robust upward trajectory, recording an average price appreciation of 10.3% year-on-year across the city. The market has absorbed over 48,700 active listings with healthy demand-supply dynamics, driven primarily by sustained IT sector hiring, infrastructure completions on the Outer Ring Road (ORR) network, and the metro Phase-II extensions now operational through Raidurg and beyond. Transaction volumes in Q2 2026 are up 14% compared to Q2 2025, with the luxury and mid-premium segments outperforming broader market expectations. New project launches in May–June 2026 alone account for approximately 6,200 units, reflecting developer confidence in sustained end-user absorption.

Western IT Corridor (HITEC City, Gachibowli, Kondapur, Madhapur)

: The western corridor remains the undisputed growth engine of Hyderabad real estate, with average prices now reaching ₹10,200–12,500 per sq ft depending on the project vintage and amenity tier. Gachibowli commands a premium due to its proximity to the financial district SEZ, where Grade-A office occupancy has crossed 94%. Kondapur and Madhapur are witnessing significant mid-rise and high-rise launches in the ₹1.2–2.8 crore ticket size, with average absorption rate of 68% within 90 days of launch. The Nanakramguda Financial District micro-market has emerged as a distinct sub-zone, with boutique luxury towers quoting ₹13,500–15,000 per sq ft — a 13% jump over June 2025 levels.

Premium Banjara Hills and Jubilee Hills Segment

: Banjara Hills and Jubilee Hills continue to anchor Hyderabad's ultra-premium segment, with limited new inventory keeping prices firm at ₹14,000–19,500 per sq ft for newly delivered projects. Road No. 36 in Jubilee Hills saw a landmark plotted development launch at ₹22,000 per sq yd, fully subscribed within three weeks. The luxury villa segment in Jubilee Hills' gated enclaves posted a 9.8% appreciation, while resale apartments in Banjara Hills Roads 10–12 are moving at ₹12,800–15,200 per sq ft with negotiation margins shrinking to under 3%. Demand here is predominantly driven by legacy wealth, NRI buyers, and senior corporate professionals seeking lifestyle upgrades, with rental yields holding at 2.8–3.4%.

Mid-Segment Localities (Miyapur, Bachupally, Kukatpally, Nizampet)

: The mid-segment belt northwest of HITEC City is witnessing the highest volume activity in absolute transaction count. Miyapur and Bachupally are recording average prices of ₹5,800–7,200 per sq ft for new launches, with projects in the ₹55–90 lakh range seeing fastest velocity. GHMC's Bachupally ward infrastructure upgrades completed in early 2026 have unlocked several previously constrained project sites. Kukatpally's KPHB colony corridor is benefiting from metro connectivity, with 2BHK units in the ₹65–85 lakh range commanding strong rental demand from IT workforce unable to afford western corridor pricing. Nizampet has seen a 12.1% price increase, the highest in this segment, as land bank scarcity begins to bite.

Emerging Areas and Growth Corridors (Kokapet, Narsingi, Adibatla, Shamshabad)

: Kokapet stands out as June 2026's hottest locality, with prices breaching ₹9,800 per sq ft on select projects — a staggering 18.4% jump over June 2025 — driven by the Kokapet Government Land auction outcomes and the Financial District spillover. Narsingi is following at ₹7,200–8,400 per sq ft with several integrated township launches. On the southeastern ORR, Adibatla's aerospace and defence manufacturing cluster is catalysing a new residential micro-market with prices at ₹4,200–5,600 per sq ft, attracting early investors. The Shamshabad–Rajendranagar corridor, boosted by RGIA expansion cargo city plans, is recording ₹4,800–6,100 per sq ft for plotted development.

Investment Recommendations

: For capital appreciation plays, Kokapet and Narsingi offer the best near-term upside (12–18 month horizon) given infrastructure tailwinds and limited supply pipeline relative to demand. Long-term investors should accumulate plotted inventory in Adibatla and Tukkuguda before Phase-III ORR service road completions, expected Q1 2027, catalyse another re-rating. Rental yield seekers should focus on 2BHK units in Miyapur and Bachupally where gross yields of 4.0–4.5% are achievable. Avoid over-leveraged bets in Jubilee Hills and Banjara Hills in the short term, as limited new inventory means liquidity risk on exit. Overall, Hyderabad continues to offer one of the best risk-adjusted real estate investment profiles among Indian metro cities in June 2026, underpinned by diversified economic drivers, investor-friendly regulatory environment, and a structural undersupply in the ₹80 lakh–1.5 crore segment.
